Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

PandaBot is a Discord bot that adds song saving, now-playing cards and music stats to servers that use a music bot such as Jockie Music. This policy explains what PandaBot stores, why, and how to delete it. PandaBot is run by an independent developer ("we") and is not affiliated with Discord or Jockie Music.

What PandaBot reads

PandaBot can see messages in the channels it has access to. It uses that to:

  • read the posts of the music bots a server follows ("Now playing", "Added Track"),
  • notice its own commands (such as p!stats and !like), and
  • notice music-bot commands (such as m!play) so a song can be credited to whoever requested it.

It does not store the text of people's messages, only music-bot posts as listed below. It also sees who is in a voice channel, but it never joins voice and never hears or records audio.

How it's used and shared

Data is used only to provide PandaBot's features. It is never sold or used for advertising. Other members of a server can see stats about that server through PandaBot's commands, including what you requested and how many songs you saved. Data from one server is not shown in another, except that /forgetme works across all of them.

PandaBot runs on Render (in the United States), which stores the database and logs on our behalf. Messages and DMs travel through Discord, under Discord's privacy policy.

How long it's kept

Saved songs, play history and listening records are kept until they are deleted as described below, so stats and your saved list keep working. Daily backups may hold deleted data for up to 14 days.

Deleting your data

  • Everything about you: run /forgetme in any server with PandaBot. After you confirm, it deletes your saved songs (and their DM cards), the record of songs you heard in voice and your DM preference in every server, and removes your name and ID from songs you requested. It can't be undone.
  • One song: tap its ❤️ again, press "Remove from list" on its DM card, or use /unlike.
  • A whole server's data: a server admin can ask us using the contact below.

Children

PandaBot is for Discord users, and Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires). We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Open source

PandaBot's code is public at github.com/uncSoft/pandabot under the GNU AGPL-3.0, so you can check exactly what it stores and how.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in p!help.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: join the PandaBot support server and message the developer there.
